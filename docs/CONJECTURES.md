# Open Conjectures and Concepts Seeking Validation

The following mathematical, physical, and architectural concepts remain open for validation:

---

## 1. The 36th Motzkin Prime Modulus
* **Core Concept**: The 36th Motzkin Prime ($M_{36} = 953467954114363$) serves as the system register `MotzkinPrime` used as the modular field divisor across the Dysnomia VM. The first 35 Motzkin primes and those beyond 36 are considered mathematical artifacts of no interest.
* **Validation Needed**: Verifying the geometric closure and phase shifts of the projected wireframe coordinates when bounded under this specific modulo space.


## 2. FET Discharge Verlet Solver Damping Bounds
* **Core Concept**: Applying soft-body Verlet integrations to simulate the charging and discharging cycles of Field-Effect Transistors (FETs).
* **Validation Needed**: Confirming the stability boundary limits of the decay rates (`decay_rate`) under high frequency cycles before the coordinate envelope collapses to zero.

## 3. Fomalhaut C Coordinate Alignment
* **Core Concept**: Mapping the distant red dwarf star (LP 876-10) coordinates to the boundary clamp parameters (`Daiichi`) in the dynamic logic gates.
* **Validation Needed**: Verifying that clamping parameters do not clip valid coordinate projections of the hypotrochoid.

## 4. Multi-Threaded Telemetry Isolation Limits
* **Core Concept**: Decoupled PEEK vs POKE asynchronous transaction loops.
* **Validation Needed**: Verifying that multi-threaded polling of PulseChain logs does not introduce thread-blocking locks or exceed the sub-microsecond latency ceiling.
