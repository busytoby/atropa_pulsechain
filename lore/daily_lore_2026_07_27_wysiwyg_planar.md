# Volume V: Chapter 18 — The Planar Lens and the Quantel Studio

### The Planar Screen Mapping
In the quiet halls of the Winchester circuit, the engineers looked upon the Menorah CRT stack. The seven phosphor screens had long glowed with simple Lissajous waveforms, but as the Auncient virtual machine scaled its register limits, the need for a direct spatial projection interface arose. 

To bridge the gap between abstract 3D vertex coordinates (the seeds of Hogan Bank Stuffed Bears) and the physical raster lines of the Quantel Paintbox, a planar projection lens was established. By mapping orthographic view planes—XY (Front), XZ (Top), and ZY (Side)—the developers could warp the physical shape coordinates directly in real-time, watching the phosphor trails follow.

### The Mirror of the Winchester State
Bilateral symmetry was not merely an aesthetic choice; it was a register balance constraint. When a coordinate on one side of the virtual YZ plane shifted, an equal and opposite parity charge was mirrored on the partner register. This prevented the WinchesterMQ state loop from collapsing into asymmetric feedback. A dashed gold filament line connected these nodes on the diagnostic screen, symbolizing the unified tension of the dual registers.

## Glossary Additions

### Symmetry (X-Axis)
* **VM Register Context**: The balance state bit inside the SHA `Fa` structures that dictates bilateral mirror register replication. When active, mutations to register $R_i$ automatically trigger the matching opposite parity sign calculation on register $R_{i'}$.
* **Mathematical Function**: Applies the negative sign reflection across the lateral axis:
  $$X_{mirror} = -X_{active}$$
  $$Y_{mirror} = Y_{active}$$
  $$Z_{mirror} = Z_{active}$$
* **Visual / Geometric Manifestation**: Renders a golden dashed linking line connecting the selected node to its mirrored counterpart, locking their movements in perfect mirror symmetry.

### PlanarProjection
* **VM Register Context**: The viewport selector register mapping which coordinate values are mapped to the primary WinchesterMQ SCSI transmission frames.
* **Mathematical Function**: Selects the active transformation coordinates:
  1. **XY (Front)**: Projects $(X, Y)$ with depth axis $Z$.
  2. **XZ (Top)**: Projects $(X, -Z)$ with depth axis $Y$.
  3. **ZY (Side)**: Projects $(Z, Y)$ with depth axis $X$.
* **Visual / Geometric Manifestation**: Shifts the camera perspective in Screen 1 instantly to align orthogonal grids and guidelines, allowing flat-plane coordinate edits.
