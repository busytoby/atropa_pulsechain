# The Federal Worker Simulation: Transitioning Fourier Impositions to Confirmed Citizens

Within the **Auncient** system architecture, raw mathematical waveforms must be structured and validated before they can be promoted to active workers. This lifecycle is represented by the **Federal Worker Simulation**, which maps the transition of an unauthorized Fourier imposition into a confirmed, authorized system participant.

### 1. Phase 0: The Unauthorized Fourier Imposition
The simulation begins with a raw Fourier peak value ($f_{peak}$) extracted from the spectral coordinate grid. At this stage, the candidate holds the status of `PHASE_UNAUTHORIZED_IMPOSITION`. Because it has no national identity, no Social Security Number, and no verified credentials, it cannot perform logic operations or receive Hogan Bank Saat endowments. It exists solely as mathematical noise.

### 2. Phase 1: The Audit
To begin the qualification loop, the raw Fourier peak is mapped to a deterministic DNA footprint payload. The system audits this footprint by computing its FNV-1a DNA signature hash. Upon validation, the candidate is promoted to `PHASE_AUDITED`.

### 3. Phase 2: Confirmation
The audited DNA footprint is sent to the material-to-cloth bridge. Using the `auncient_bridge_dna_to_ssa` translation, the system deterministically maps the candidate's DNA seed to a regional Social Security Number and site (e.g. seed `42` resolving to SSN `007-12-3456` in `Maine`). This maps the mathematical coordinate directly to a verified citizen identity record, promoting the candidate to `PHASE_CONFIRMED`.

### 4. Phase 3: Selection
To transition the confirmed candidate into an active worker (`PHASE_SELECTED_WORKER`), the system must grant explicit authority:
* **Hogan Registration**: The candidate's DNA footprint is registered in the bank database, opening an account with the default endowment of `1,000,000` Saat.
* **WinchesterMQ Dispatch**: The selection transaction is dispatched over the WinchesterMQ SCSI channel.
* **PKI key check**: The dispatch is blocked by the default-reject policy unless verified by a signature payload containing at least $K \ge 4$ keys.

Upon successful signature validation, the dispatcher logs the `DisplacementShader` vertex coordinate sync alignments, confirming the worker's explicit, authorized basis for work in the system.
