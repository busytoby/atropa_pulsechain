# Principle 1: Every Fourier Is An Imposition And Only Potentially Also A Worker

Within the **Auncient** system architecture, the integration of mathematical waves and active processes is governed by a core design rule:

> **Every Fourier Is An Imposition And Only Potentially Also A Worker.**

### 1. The Core Imposition
A Fourier transform is first and foremost an mathematical imposition—a projection of spatial data points onto a spectral coordinate grid. It has no national authorization, no Social Security Number, and no organic identity. It does not perform transaction processing or logic routines; it merely imposes frequency structures and phase shifts upon the system registers.

### 2. The Potential Worker
A Fourier operator only becomes a "worker" when it is coupled to a system process under strict authorization:
* **The Transition**: When the output peak variables are mapped to an active driver—such as the ALSA sound generator or the Vulkan vertex shader—the spectral coefficients begin driving physical changes in the presentation layer.
* **The Security Gate**: This transition is never automatic. Because the transform carries no intrinsic credentials, it must be authorized by EDSAC and AUTODIN at the system-wide gate before its calculations can perform work.
