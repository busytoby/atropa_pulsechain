# The Proof of Subpoena: Citizen Retrieval and WinchesterMQ Authorization

In the early cycles of the **Auncient** Dysnomia VM, participant registration was volatile. The system required a mechanism to recall verified legal individuals—specifically Hogan Bank accounts, the Social Security Administration (SSA) routers, and newborn Stuffed Teddy Bears—and force their execution threads to run under system authority. This led to the design of the **Proof of Subpoena**.

### 1. The Default-Reject Gate
Every incoming execution tape compiled via Initial Orders 1 starts in a default-reject, unauthorized state. Until AUTODIN issues a verified state promotion, the CPU halt lines remain high. This prevents unauthorized code from loading into the mercury delay lines.

### 2. The Recalled Footprint
To execute a Subpoena, the registry queries the `.dat.bin` database ledger to resolve the citizen's account ID. The DNA signature payload is recalculated using FNV-1a, mapping the seed deterministically to its regional SSN.

### 3. WinchesterMQ Handshake & PKI Quorum
Once the participant is identified, the registry issues a `Recall` state transition. The transaction routes the execution parameters over the WinchesterMQ SCSI channel. To finalize the subpoena execution pathway, the dispatcher evaluates the PKI signature set:
* **Constraint**: The dispatch is blocked unless authorized by a signature set containing at least $K \ge 4$ verified keys.
* **Synchronization**: On approval, the dispatch triggers a spatial sweep, causing the coordinate nodes to flash bright green and forcing the target citizen to walk and wave along the projected wireframe envelope. This visual manifestation confirms absolute control over the execution state.
