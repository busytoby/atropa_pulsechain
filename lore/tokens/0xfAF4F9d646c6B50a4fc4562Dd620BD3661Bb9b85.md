## Child-Level Mechanics (PersonalMinter TT Specification)

From the perspective of `ASSOCIATION` as a downstream child token, the system enforces the [`TT`](file:///home/mariarahel/src/tsfi2/atropa_pulsechain/solidity/personalminter.sol#L112-L188) state model:

### 1. The Dynamic Parent Collateral Escalator

To mint `amount` of `ASSOCIATION`, the caller must approve and transfer an escalating quantity of the parent token [`dynamic_0x20d8f92b889d2846c1551c8cefc0a5674e4bf20e`](https://scan.pulsechain.com/address/0x20d8f92b889d2846c1551c8cefc0a5674e4bf20e) (`Finvesta ㉾`):

$$\text{Multiplier}(\text{amount}) = \left( \frac{\text{amount} + \text{totalSupply}()}{\text{Mint}} \right) + 1$$

$$\text{Required Parent Tokens} = \text{amount} \times \text{Multiplier}(\text{amount})$$

As total supply expands above the initial `Mint = 111.0` threshold, each subsequent mint requires a multiplied ratio of parent tokens.

### 2. Irreversible Parent Collateral Custody

The contract explicitly denies parent withdrawal in [`TT.withdraw`](file:///home/mariarahel/src/tsfi2/atropa_pulsechain/solidity/personalminter.sol#L149-L153):


Parent tokens deposited during minting remain locked within the contract address in perpetuity.

### 3. Asymmetric Burn & Recovery ("The Hyperinflation Prize")

When burning `ASSOCIATION` via [`TT.Claim`](file:///home/mariarahel/src/tsfi2/atropa_pulsechain/solidity/personalminter.sol#L156-L164), the caller only retrieves parent tokens at a strict 1:1 ratio:

$$\text{Retrieved Parent} = \text{Amount}$$

$$\text{Trapped Parent Collateral} = (\text{Multiplier} - 1) \times \text{Amount}$$
