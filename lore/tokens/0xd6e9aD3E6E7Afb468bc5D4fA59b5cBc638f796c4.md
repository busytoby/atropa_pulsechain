## IndexMinter Contract Mechanics ([`indexminter.sol`](file:///home/mariarahel/src/tsfi2/atropa_pulsechain/solidity/indexminter.sol))

### 1. Macro-Scale Base Divisor Multiplier

In the IndexMinter implementation of [`TT.Multiplier`](file:///home/mariarahel/src/tsfi2/atropa_pulsechain/solidity/indexminter.sol#L87-L89), the divisor is scaled to a macro threshold:

$$\text{Multiplier}(\text{addition}) = \left( \frac{\text{addition} + \text{totalSupply}()}{1{,}111{,}111{,}111 \times 10^{18}} \right) + 1$$

To mint new `BODYGUARD` tokens, the caller must transfer:

$$\text{Required Parent Tokens} = \text{amount} \times \text{Multiplier}(\text{amount})$$

where the parent asset is [`dynamic_0x84d317737611feb25ec3207fba5945f3cb44c143`](https://scan.pulsechain.com/address/0x84d317737611feB25Ec3207fBa5945f3cb44C143) (`IPO ㋨`).

### 2. Debenture Enforcement & Cross-Contract Claiming

* **Debenture Lock**: The contract initializes with `Debenture = true`. If any non-parent token is extracted via [`TT.withdraw`](file:///home/mariarahel/src/tsfi2/atropa_pulsechain/solidity/indexminter.sol#L102-L108), `Debenture` is permanently disabled (`false`), permanently locking redemption.
* **Cross-Contract Claim**: Under [`TT.Claim`](file:///home/mariarahel/src/tsfi2/atropa_pulsechain/solidity/indexminter.sol#L111-L124), the caller can burn tokens from this contract or any sister contract sharing the same `Creator` and `Parent` to retrieve underlying [`dynamic_0x84d317737611feb25ec3207fba5945f3cb44c143`](https://scan.pulsechain.com/address/0x84d317737611feB25Ec3207fBa5945f3cb44C143) (`IPO ㋨`) parent tokens at a 1:1 ratio.
