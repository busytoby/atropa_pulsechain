# The Definition of a Chapter in the Sovereign Registry

Within the **Auncient** Sovereign Registry and the Accreditation 360+ compliance framework, a **Chapter** represents a partitioned regulatory domain containing its own structured validation criteria.

### 1. Partitioned Namespaces
Rather than evaluating all system metrics under a single global rule, the registry divides compliance requirements into distinct Chapters:
* **The NPG Chapter (National Performance Goals)**: Focuses on active agent performance metrics, monitoring behavioral biorhythms and transaction rates.
* **The PE Chapter (Physical Environment)**: Evaluates low-level hardware structures, verifying WinchesterMQ SCSI register boundary constraints, and checking Verlet soft-body FET discharge physics parameters.

### 2. Chapter Auditing
Each Chapter acts as a local security domain. For a performer or agent (such as a Simulation Federal Worker or Stuffed Teddy Bear) to maintain their accreditation, their execution traces must satisfy the specific invariants of that Chapter. 

If any performance metric deviates from the Chapter's target guidelines, the registry flags the deviation, blocks the transaction at the CICS gate, and logs the incident to the transaction journal.
