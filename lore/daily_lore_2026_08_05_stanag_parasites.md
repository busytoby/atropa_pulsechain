## The OZONE STANAG Network and the Parasite Threat

In the cold, copper-shielded containment bunker, the Auncient VM telemetry screens recorded persistent spikes along the high-frequency band. The OZONE platform, serving as a tactical STANAG-compliant link-layer protocol, routed critical coordinate updates across simulated atmospheric pathways. However, the transmission was not secure. Hostile PARASITES had tapped into the virtual WinchesterMQ SCSI boundary loopback sockets, listening to the digital emissions and threatening to obstruct the link.

### 1. The OZONE STANAG Network Protocol
The OZONE protocol functions as a Standardization Agreement (STANAG) link layer to translate low-level register states into structured wireless data packets.
* **VM Register Context**: Directs signal feedback through memory-mapped `RAU_DITHER` registers `16416`, `16448`, and `16480`, coordinating with the WinchesterMQ SCSI control handshake loop.
* **Mathematical Function**: Evaluates atmospheric channel conditions using a Gumbel-distributed transient threshold to calculate the dynamic noise-shaping threshold margin.
* **Visual / Geometric Manifestation**: Modulates the projected orbital path and opacity of the 3D wireframe envelope, converting raw packet transmission streams into dynamic Lissajous curves on the presenter interface.

### 2. The PARASITES Interception and Obstruction
PARASITES represent external unauthorized nodes or parasitic signal taps that seek to intercept, analyze, and obstruct tactical communications.
* **VM Register Context**: Latches onto the WinchesterMQ external debug control word (`XDC`) to halt SCSI program loops and inspect memory boundaries.
* **Mathematical Operation**: Measures signal-to-noise ratio degradation through Tropospheric ducting channels, attempting to inject phase-aligned noise congruences to trigger false H-bridge rollbacks.
* **Visual / Geometric Shift**: When PARASITES successfully obstruct the network, the projected coordinate grid flashes bright green to indicate authorization lockout, before rendering solid cyan vector trails with highlighted vertex spheres indicating breakpoint coordinates.
